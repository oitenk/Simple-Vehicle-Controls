# Simple Vehicle Controls

A small panel for Cyberpunk 2077 that controls your car with the mouse: engine, driver and passenger doors, driver and passenger windows, hood and trunk. It shows a simple top-down car; click a part to open or close it.

Built it with Claude (the AI).

## Use

Press the hotkey (Numpad 5 by default) in a car, or while looking at one. Click parts of the car, or the buttons. Press the key again or Esc to close. The key can be changed in Mod Settings.

Every control can also have its own key in Mod Settings (none are bound by default).

## Requirements

- [redscript](https://github.com/jac3km4/redscript)
- [Codeware](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-codeware)
- [Mod Settings](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/4885) (optional, for changing the key)

## Install

Install the zip with Vortex, or extract it into the game folder.
